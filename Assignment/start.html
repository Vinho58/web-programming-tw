01 / DISCOVER

The city of big dreams.

MORE THAN A CITY

New York is big and loud. There is always something to see, like Times Square or Central Park.

The Empire State Building is one of the most famous buildings in the city. From the top you can see all of New York.

"New York is best seen on foot."

02 / NEIGHBORHOODS

Every part is different.

THE OTHER SIDE

New York is not only Manhattan. Brooklyn has small cafes, old brick houses and a lot of street art. On the weekend there are markets where you can try food from all over the world.

Take your time and walk around. Every neighborhood looks and feels a little different. In one street you hear music, in the next one it is quiet and green.

In the evening, the bridges light up and you have a great view of Manhattan. Many people sit by the water and watch the skyline.

"Sometimes the best places are the ones you find by accident."
